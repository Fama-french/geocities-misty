ABOUT MISTY

Misty was a moggy, born to our cat Nougatine (also a moggy) — we never knew who the father was. She loved cuddles and loved wandering around, and she'd make the most beautiful noise when you scratched her. She'd curl up right next to you, or climb onto your lap hoping for a taste of butter. She isn't with us anymore, but she'll always be part of the family and this page will always be hers.
